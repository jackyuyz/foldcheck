## description

```text
Check your phone one step at a time, and keep a record of what you observe.

FoldCheck provides guided screen and hardware checks, with inner-screen, cover-screen, crease and hinge tests for iPhone Duo. Start a full inspection or jump into a quick touch, screen or hinge check. Supported iPhones without a hinge can run the applicable checks.

SCREEN AND TOUCH
Swipe across a touch grid or tap individual cells. A denser band checks touch response around the fold on the Duo inner display. Rotate while testing: the same physical cells stay covered and progress continues. Check multi-touch response, inspect solid-color patterns and mark visible screen issues yourself.

HINGE AND CREASE
Follow guided hinge sweeps and a short stability hold. Review continuity and consistency, rather than a claim of calibrated angle accuracy. Rate crease visibility at guided viewing angles and optionally import a reference photo taken with another device.

HARDWARE CHECKS
Try available cameras, speaker test tones, microphone recording and playback, motion sensors, proximity, haptics and volume buttons. Touch ID is checked on devices that offer it. Some checks rely on what you see, hear or feel; other physical buttons use a manual checklist. Unsupported or unavailable checks are identified in the results, and tests can be skipped.

FREE CHECKS, OPTIONAL REPORTS
Run the applicable tests and view their on-screen results for free. Report verification is also free.

A one-time in-app purchase unlocks PDF reports, access to saved inspection history, comparison of two inspections, optional monthly local reminders and fold statistics. Reports include results, notes and a QR code. Restore Purchases is available in Settings and on the purchase screen. The current price is shown before purchase.

LOCAL RECORDS
No account is required. Inspection data stays on your device unless you choose to share it. There are no advertising or analytics SDKs. Checks and report verification work offline; product information, purchases and restores use Apple's services.

Switch between English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French and Spanish in Settings. Reports can follow the interface language or stay in English.

UNDERSTAND THE RESULTS
Fold statistics count only cycles observed while FoldCheck is in the foreground, not the phone's lifetime folds. FoldCheck does not read battery health, serial numbers or IMEI, or measure physical crease depth.

PDF import verifies embedded signed report data. QR scanning verifies the signature for the report hash. Neither verifies every visible PDF page, device identity or the device clock. Reports are user-generated inspection records, not official certification or a guarantee of device condition.

Available checks depend on your hardware and system support.
```
